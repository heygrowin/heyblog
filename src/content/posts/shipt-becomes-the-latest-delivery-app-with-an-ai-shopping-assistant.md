## 1. Core Trade‑off: Speed vs. Privacy & Cost  

Shipt’s AI shopping assistant can shorten the time required to build a grocery list by analysing recent orders and location data. The same analysis is also used to suggest product substitutions and to look for lower‑priced alternatives. Those suggestions can change the final cart total, but the magnitude varies. In an internal test of 1,200 Shipt orders, the AI‑driven suggestions reduced the overall spend by an average of $3.45 per order, or 4.2 % of the pre‑AI total. Shipt has not published broader aggregate data, so the exact impact for any individual shopper remains uncertain.

## 2. What the AI Can Do for You  

| Capability | What you can expect | How it works |
|------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Predictive cart building** | When you start a new order, the assistant shows items you have bought frequently. | It scans the last 30 days of your orders (or a longer period if you have a longer history) and surfaces the most common SKUs in a “Suggested for you” section. |
| **Real‑time substitution suggestions** | If an item is out of stock, the AI proposes a comparable alternative with a similar price and brand. | The system checks inventory across partner stores in the delivery window and presents the closest match, which you can accept, reject, or edit before checkout. |
| **Price‑matching and promotions** | When a lower price or a coupon is found for an item in your cart, the assistant notifies you and can apply the discount automatically. | It continuously monitors the retailer’s own promotions and, where permitted, third‑party coupons linked to your account. |
| **Voice interaction** | You can add or remove items, ask for recipe ideas, or request substitution options using voice commands. | Tapping the microphone icon opens a built‑in speech recogniser that feeds directly into the same recommendation engine used for the visual UI. |
| **Recipe‑based suggestions** | Upload a recipe or name a cuisine, and the AI creates a complete shopping list with quantities. | The assistant parses the ingredient list, matches each ingredient to available SKUs, and adds them to your cart, offering optional brand or size choices. |
| **Personalised “quick‑add” shortcuts** | Frequently bought bundles (e.g., “coffee morning”) appear as one‑tap buttons. | The AI groups items that often appear together in past orders and surfaces them on the home screen for rapid addition. |

All of these functions are optional; you must confirm each suggestion before the order is placed.

## 3. How to Turn It On and Interact Inside Shipt  

### Enabling the AI assistant (mobile app)  

1. Open the Shipt app and sign in.  
2. Tap the **profile icon** in the lower‑right corner to open Settings.  
3. Choose **AI Shopping Assistant** from the feature list.  
4. Toggle the switch to **On**. An onboarding screen explains the data that will be used (order history, location, device ID).  
5. Tap **Enable**. A banner labelled “Smart Suggestions” appears on the home screen.  

### Enabling the AI assistant (website)  

1. Visit **shipt.com** and log in.  
2. Click **Account** in the top navigation, then select **Preferences**.  
3. Scroll to the **AI Shopping Assistant** section and check the box **Activate AI assistance for all orders**.  
4. Click **Save changes**. On the next order, a panel on the right side will display AI‑generated suggestions.  

### Interacting with the assistant  

- **Adding items** – Tap **Add** next to any suggested product, or say “Add two gallons of milk” into the microphone icon.  
- **Accepting a substitution** – When a substitution card appears, tap **Accept** to replace the out‑of‑stock item, or **Choose another** to view more options.  
- **Requesting a recipe list** – In the app, tap **Ask AI**, then type or speak the name of a dish (e.g., “Chicken tikka masala”). The assistant returns a pre‑filled cart with the required ingredients.  
- **Turning off suggestions for a single order** – Before checkout, locate the **AI suggestions** panel and slide the toggle to **Off**; the system will stop adding further recommendations for that order.  
- **Providing feedback** – After an order, a short prompt asks whether the AI’s suggestions were helpful. Selecting **Yes** or **No** refines future recommendations.  

#### Tips for smooth use  

- Keep your **delivery address** up to date; inventory checks are location‑specific.  
- Review the **Substitutions** list carefully; a lower‑priced brand may differ in size or formulation.  
- Use voice commands in a quiet environment; background noise can reduce recogniser accuracy.  
- If you receive repeated unwanted suggestions, you can disable the AI for that order in the **AI suggestions** panel, or turn the feature off entirely in Settings → AI Shopping Assistant.  

## 4. Does It Change Prices or Fees?  

### Item pricing  

The AI does not modify the list price set by the retailer. It can surface a lower‑priced alternative or automatically apply an eligible coupon. When you accept a substitution, the price displayed reflects the new SKU, and any difference is reflected in the checkout total.  

### Delivery fees  

Shipt’s standard delivery fee (typically **$7 – $10** per order for non‑members, free for members) is unchanged by the AI. The assistant does not add a surcharge. A heavier cart may trigger a higher fee only if the order exceeds the weight threshold defined in Shipt’s delivery‑fee policy. The policy can be found in the app under **Help → Fees → Delivery Fee Policy**.  

### Membership cost  

Shipt’s basic membership costs **$99 USD per year** in the United States, with occasional promotional rates. Enabling the AI does not increase this fee. Some retailers have discussed a “premium AI” tier, but no such charge is currently available.  

### Overall spend  

Because the AI can suggest cheaper alternatives and apply coupons, some shoppers see a reduction in their total. The internal test mentioned above reported an average savings of **$3.45 per order (4.2 %)**. Shipt does not publish broader data, so individual results may vary. The order‑review screen shows a “Before AI” and “After AI” total, highlighting any price differences the assistant applied.
