## Decision Framework – Pick the Factor That Matters Most  

When selecting a time‑tracking app for personal use, three practical dimensions usually drive the choice:

| Factor | What to consider | Typical trade‑offs |
|--------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Platform coverage** | Does the app run on the devices you use (mobile, desktop, web)? | Apps with broader platform support may have a larger feature set but can involve cloud sync that raises privacy questions. |
| **Privacy & offline capability** | Where is your data stored? Can you work without an internet connection? | Offline‑first tools keep data on the device, limiting exposure, but they often lack automatic integrations. |
| **Automation & integrations** | Does the app automatically capture activity or connect to calendars, task managers, or APIs? | Automation saves manual entry time, yet it may require cloud processing and a paid plan for full access. |
| **Cost** | What does the free tier include, and how much does the next‑level plan cost? | Free plans can be sufficient for basic tracking; paid plans unlock advanced reports, unlimited projects, or premium support. |

Identify which column is the most critical for your workflow, then refer to the matrix below for a recommended app that aligns with that priority. If two factors are equally important, compare the corresponding apps side‑by‑side in the detailed tables that follow.
