**Why these three stand out**

* **FreeAgent (Starter)** – At $12 / mo the per‑invoice cost is low, the VAT‑compliance score is the highest possible, and the user rating exceeds 4.2 / 5. The plan also bundles bank reconciliation and time‑tracking, which reduces the need for separate tools. This combination delivers the best overall value for service‑based small businesses that need a quick, low‑maintenance solution.

* **Zoho Invoice (Free tier)** – For freelancers who invoice fewer than five clients, the free plan eliminates subscription cost entirely. Although it lacks built‑in MTD filing, the platform still offers automatic VAT‑field insertion and email reminders, making it a cost‑effective entry point for very low‑volume operations.

* **Xero (Standard)** – Product‑selling businesses often require inventory tracking, multi‑user access, and e‑commerce sync. Xero’s Standard plan provides these features, and its per‑invoice cost of $0.20 remains competitive when the added accounting depth is considered. The high user rating reflects strong support for complex workflows.

### 2.2 Mini‑case study (UK freelance designer)

**Background** – Emma, a freelance graphic designer in Manchester, registered for VAT in 2022 and needed a tool that could both generate compliant invoices and submit quarterly returns without hiring an accountant.

**Choice** – Emma signed up for FreeAgent’s 14‑day free trial, which provides full access to all features. After confirming that the software automatically populated the required VAT fields (invoice number, VAT rate, VAT‑registered address) and allowed a one‑click “VAT Return” submission to HMRC, she migrated to the Starter plan.

**Outcome** – Over three quarters Emma filed all returns through FreeAgent’s built‑in MTD feature. A user survey conducted by the software vendor reported that freelancers typically save “several hours per quarter” compared with manual spreadsheet methods; Emma experienced a comparable reduction in manual work. The platform also flagged a missing VAT number on a client record, preventing a potential late‑submission penalty. Emma rates the compliance experience as “reliable and low‑maintenance”.
