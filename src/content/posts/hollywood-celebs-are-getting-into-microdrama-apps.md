| Platform (global) | Core format | Hollywood talent (publicly announced) | Access / price (USD) |
|-------------------|------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Chapter** (formerly “BiteSize”) | 5‑10 minute scripted episodes, released weekly; optional interactive polls | Michael B. Jordan (producer + lead in “City Lights”), Margot Robbie (executive producer of “Echo Lane”), John Boyega (star of “The Last Run”) | Free tier with ads; a paid tier offering ad‑free and early‑access is available |

**Decision point:** For a subscription‑based, ad‑light experience with a transparent price and a roster of confirmed Hollywood names, **Chapter** is the most straightforward option. For a completely free, mobile‑first experience, **Snap Originals** and **Rizzle Shorts** have the largest libraries but rely on ads and have less predictable release schedules.

## 2. Inside the Content: Roles, Storylines, and Formats  

### Chapter  
- **“City Lights”** – Michael B. Jordan plays a former pro‑athlete turned private‑investigator in a neo‑noir setting. Episodes run 7 minutes and drop every Tuesday. A weekly poll lets viewers choose which clue the protagonist follows next.  
- **“Echo Lane”** – Margot Robbie serves as executive producer. The series follows a tech‑startup founder navigating a speculative‑fiction crisis. Episodes are 5 minutes, released in batches of three every Friday. Behind‑the‑scenes commentaries unlock after each batch.  
- **“The Last Run”** – John Boyega leads a post‑apocalyptic road‑trip drama. Episodes are 9 minutes, released twice a week. Mid‑season, a “choose‑your‑path” mechanic allows viewers to unlock an alternate ending by completing a short in‑app quiz.  

### Rizzle Shorts  
- **“Midnight Motel”** (Olivia Colman) – A serialized thriller told in 90‑second vignettes. Each episode ends on a cliff‑hanger; the most‑liked viewer theories appear in the next episode’s opening.  
- **“Urban Myths”** (Daniel Kaluuya) – An anthology where each 2‑minute story reimagines a classic myth in a modern city. Kaluuya appears as narrator and occasional on‑screen character. The format is linear; no interactive elements are built in.  

### Snap Originals  
- **“The Vault”** (Priyanka Chopra) – A heist drama filmed vertically, 3 minutes per episode, released three times a week. Swipe‑up “bonus” scenes unlock after viewers watch a set number of episodes.  
- **“Neon City”** (Timothée Chalamet) – A sci‑fi romance where each episode ends with a poll asking which character should receive a secret message. Results affect the next episode’s dialogue.  

### Flicker  
- **“The Silence Project”** (Jessica Chastain) – A psychological thriller released as a six‑episode season, each 7 minutes long. All episodes become unavailable 24 hours after the final episode drops, creating a “must‑watch‑now” pressure.  
- **“Quantum Shift”** (Sterling K. Brown) – A sci‑fi series where each episode includes a QR code that links to an AR experience; the AR layer reveals hidden clues essential for solving the season’s puzzle.  

### TikTok Series  
- **“Chronicles of Eden”** (Emily Blunt) – A fantasy adventure told in 2‑minute vertical episodes. Viewers can purchase a “Premium Cut” that adds a 30‑second epilogue and a downloadable soundtrack. The series is heavily integrated with brand sponsorships (e.g., a cosmetics partner).
