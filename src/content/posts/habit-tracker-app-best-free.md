## Which free habit‑tracker apps give the most features without hidden costs?  

| App | Core free features* | Main limitation after the free tier |
|-----|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Loop Habit Tracker** (Android, web) | Unlimited habits, flexible reminders (time‑of‑day, day‑of‑week, interval), bar/line/heat‑map charts, CSV export, no ads | No built‑in cloud sync; premium‑only widgets on Android |
| **Habitica** (iOS, Android, web) | Unlimited habits, daily/weekly reminders, streak tracking, quests, guilds, cloud sync, JSON export | Cosmetic items and some advanced stats require “gems” bought with real money |
| **HabitBull** (iOS, Android) | Unlimited habit streaks, up to three daily reminders per habit, line chart and 30‑day heat‑map, cloud backup | Free version caps active habits at five and shows banner ads; CSV export is premium‑only |
| **TickTick** (iOS, Android, web, macOS, Windows) | Unlimited habits, basic line and bar charts, unlimited reminders, cross‑device cloud sync, no ads | Premium needed for custom habit colors, advanced statistics, calendar view, and CSV/PDF export |
| **Productive – Habit Tracker** (iOS, Android) | Unlimited habits, push notifications, habit‑stacking, simple streak count, occasional non‑intrusive ads | Premium removes ads and adds habit templates and CSV export |

\*Features listed are those available without a paid subscription as of August 2026.

### Quick take  
The apps that provide the most functionality for free are **Loop Habit Tracker**, **TickTick**, and **Habitica**. Loop offers the most detailed charts without ads. TickTick supplies unlimited reminders and cross‑device sync, though its calendar view is a premium feature. Habitica adds a gamified community at no cost, while keeping core tracking free.

## Privacy and data‑security considerations for the free versions  

| App | Where data is stored | Export option | Advertising / tracking SDKs | Notable privacy points |
|-----|----------------------|---------------|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| Loop Habit Tracker | Local SQLite database on device; optional sync via user‑configured services (e.g., Nextcloud, Google Drive) | CSV export from Settings → Export → CSV | None | Open‑source (MIT licence); code auditable on GitHub |
| Habitica | Cloud storage on AWS (US‑based) under Habitica’s own servers | JSON export via account settings | Google Analytics on the website only (no ads in app) | Optional two‑factor authentication (2FA); data subject to Habitica’s privacy policy |
| HabitBull | Proprietary cloud servers (US) linked to a HabitBull account | CSV export requires premium subscription | Google AdMob for banner ads; Firebase Analytics | Privacy policy mentions data may be used for service improvement; no explicit resale clause |
| TickTick | Cloud servers (US) managed by TickTick Ltd. | Premium users can export CSV/PDF; free users can request a data export from support | Mixpanel for usage analytics (opt‑out available in Settings) | 2FA supported; GDPR‑compliant statements for EU users |
| Productive | Cloud servers (US) operated by Productive Ltd. | CSV export only with premium | Firebase Analytics; banner ads in free tier | Privacy policy allows data use for personalized ads; analytics can be disabled in Settings |

### Quick take  
If keeping data on the device is a priority, **Loop Habit Tracker** is the only free app that does so by default and does not embed advertising SDKs. **Habitica** stores data in the cloud but offers optional 2FA and no ads. The other apps either display ads or rely on third‑party analytics, which may be a concern for privacy‑focused users.
