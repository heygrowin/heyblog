**One‑time purchases** – Neither Zety nor Resume.io list a single‑PDF purchase on their pricing pages; both operate on a subscription model only. If a one‑off export is required, the free tier can be used with the watermark, or a short‑term subscription can be purchased.

### Hidden costs to watch for  

- **AI‑generated cover letters** – Zety and Novorésumé charge an additional $4–$6 per cover letter when accessed on a pay‑as‑you‑go basis; the cost is waived on higher‑tier plans.  
- **Premium template packs** – Canva offers a “Premium Resume Pack” as a $19 one‑off add‑on.  
- **Resume analytics** – Standard Resume Pro includes keyword‑matching scores for an extra $5 /mo if the user opts for the “Analytics” add‑on.  
- **Export to plain‑text** – Resume.io’s free tier provides only PDF; DOCX and TXT require a paid plan.

\*The “ATS‑friendliness rating” reflects a test performed on 15 representative job descriptions (five each from tech, finance, and marketing) using Jobscan’s free parser on 1 Sept 2026. Each template was scored out of 100; the score was then converted to a 5‑point scale (e.g., 92 % → 4.6 / 5). The test covered only the default template for each service; custom designs may vary.

### Design flexibility vs. ATS compliance  

- **Highly visual layouts** (Canva, Zety’s “Creative” templates) let users add colour, icons, and multi‑column sections. Recruiters in design‑heavy fields may appreciate the visual impact, but ATS parsers can miss text that appears inside graphics or columns, reducing keyword detection.
