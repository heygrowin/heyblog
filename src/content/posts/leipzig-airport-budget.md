## 2. Getting There & Away – Public Transport and Driving

### By train (S‑Bahn)

- **Line**: S2 of the S‑Bahn Mitteldeutschland network.  
- **Stops**: Leipzig Hauptbahnhof → Leipzig/Halle Airport (terminal stop).  
- **Travel time**: ~15 min from Leipzig Hbf, ~12 min from Halle (Saale) Hbf.  
- **Frequency**: Every 30 min on weekdays, hourly on weekends (subject to change).  
- **Ticket price (2024)**: a standard regional fare applies for journeys covering both Leipzig and Halle zones. Tickets are sold at DB machines, via the DB Navigator app, or at the station kiosk.  

A rough annual cost for two round trips per month can be estimated based on the standard fare. A regional “Mitteldeutschland” pass is available and may become cheaper for frequent travelers.

### By bus

| Bus line | Route | Frequency | Travel time | Fare (2024) |
|----------|-------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| 10 | Airport ↔ Leipzig Hbf (via Leipzig‑Mitte) | Every 30 min (Mon‑Sat) | 30 min | Standard regional fare |
| 20 | Airport ↔ Halle Hbf | Every 30 min (Mon‑Sat) | 25 min | Standard regional fare |
| N9 (night) | Airport ↔ Leipzig Hbf (late night) | Hourly (Fri‑Sat) | 35 min | Standard regional fare |

The same regional ticket used for the S‑Bahn is valid on these buses, so a single purchase covers either mode for a given journey.

### Taxi and rideshare

- **Taxi** – Metered fares based on distance and time; rates differ between Leipzig and Halle city centres and may include night surcharges.  
- **Rideshare (e.g., Uber, Bolt)** – Fares are calculated according to each provider’s pricing structure and may vary with demand.  

If a traveler uses a taxi or rideshare regularly, the yearly expense will depend on the actual trips taken. Promotions on rideshare apps can reduce costs.

### Driving yourself

- **From Leipzig** – Take the A14 northbound, exit 4 (Leipzig/Halle Airport). Distance ≈ 30 km; travel time 25–30 min under normal traffic.  
- **From Halle** – Take the A14 southbound, exit 4. Distance ≈ 20 km; travel time 20–25 min.  

**Fuel cost estimate** (2024 diesel price and typical midsize car consumption) suggests a modest expense for a round trip; the total annual cost depends on the number of trips taken. The A14 has no tolls; parking fees are covered in the next section.

### How to pre‑book

1. Open the official parking portal: `https://www.leipzig-halle-airport.de/en/parking`.  
2. Choose the zone, entry date, and departure date.  
3. Review the calculated total (any applicable discounts are applied automatically).  
4. Pay with credit card or PayPal; a confirmation email containing a QR code is sent.  
5. Display the QR code at the entry barrier; the gate lifts automatically.  

### Cost example

A business traveler who parks in the Premium zone for 10 days each month (120 days per year) pays a total based on the published daily rate and any applicable monthly discounts.  

If the same traveler used the Long‑term zone for the same 120 days, the annual cost would be lower, reflecting the trade‑off between convenience and price.

*Note:* Rates may be adjusted for holidays or special events. Verify current prices before booking.

## 4. Passenger Amenities – Connectivity, Lounges, Food, Shops, Services

### Connectivity

- **Wi‑Fi** – Available throughout the terminal; users accept a terms‑of‑service screen. Reported download speeds are sufficient for email, messaging and standard‑quality streaming.  
- **Paid upgrade** – Higher‑speed access is offered for an additional fee, with pricing set by the provider. Most travelers find the basic tier sufficient.

### Lounges

| Lounge | Operator | Access | Opening hours |
|--------|----------|--------|---------------|
| Lufthansa Business Lounge | Lufthansa | Access for eligible passengers or via day‑pass purchase where available | 06:00 – 22:00 |
| Airport Lounge (independent) | Airport | Day passes are available for purchase or via lounge‑access programs | 06:30 – 20:30 |
| Micro‑lounges | Various low‑cost carriers | Generally not provided; passengers use the public seating area. |

Lounges offer complimentary drinks, light snacks, workstations and shower facilities. No dedicated lounge exists for Ryanair or Wizz Air.

### Food & beverage

- **Fast‑food** – McDonald’s (counter service, drive‑through style).  
- **Sit‑down options** – A small selection of German and international cafés, a bakery, and a restaurant serving regional dishes. Opening hours align with terminal operations; most outlets close after the last flight departure.

### Retail and services

- **Shops** – Duty‑free (when applicable), souvenir store, newsstand, and a small electronics kiosk.  
- **Currency exchange** – One bureau de change located near the main security checkpoint; rates follow market levels and include a modest commission.  
- **Accessibility** – The terminal is equipped with step‑free routes, elevators, tactile guidance paths, and accessible restrooms. Assistance for passengers with reduced mobility can be requested
