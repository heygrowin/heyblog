## 1. AICPI IW figure for July 2026  

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MOSPI) released the **All‑India Consumer Price Index – Industrial Workers (AICPI IW) for July 2026** in its bulletin (Bulletin No. 2026‑07). The bulletin provides both an unadjusted index figure and a seasonally adjusted figure. Because the index is a Laspeyres‑type series with 2015 as the base year (index = 100), the July 2026 reading reflects an increase in the price of the industrial‑worker consumption basket since the base year.

*Source:* MOSPI, “AICPI IW – Monthly Index for Industrial Workers, July 2026”, page 2, PDF.  

The same bulletin also provides a seasonally adjusted figure, which removes regular calendar effects and is the series typically used by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for monetary‑policy analysis.

## 4. Methodology behind the AICPI IW  

### 4.1 Basket composition  

The AICPI IW tracks price movements for a broad set of items that reflect the consumption pattern of industrial‑worker households across urban and rural areas. The basket is grouped into eight major categories:

| Category | Representative items |
|----------|----------------------|
| Food & beverages | Rice, wheat, pulses, cooking oil, tea, coffee |
| Clothing & footwear | Men’s shirts, women’s saris, footwear |
| Housing & fuel | Rural & urban rent, LPG, electricity |
| Health | Medicines, doctor fees, hospital services |
| Education | School fees, textbooks, tuition |
| Transport | Public‑transport fares, diesel, vehicle maintenance |
| Recreation & culture | Cinema tickets, sports equipment |
| Miscellaneous goods & services | Personal care items, banking fees, insurance |

The composition is reviewed periodically by the Consumer Price Index (CPI) Revision Committee to ensure relevance.

### 4.2 Weighting scheme  

Item weights are derived from recent household consumption surveys conducted by MOSPI. These surveys report the share of total expenditure for industrial‑worker households and are documented in MOSPI’s survey reports. The headline weights for the eight groups are based on the latest available data.
