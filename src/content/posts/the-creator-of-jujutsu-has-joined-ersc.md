## 2. What is ERSC?  

ERSC is an abbreviation for a fan‑driven Elder Scrolls role‑playing group (sometimes rendered as *Elder Scrolls Role‑playing Community*). It is a fan‑driven organization that supports tabletop and live‑action role‑playing games set in the world of Bethesda’s *The Elder Scrolls* franchise.  

The society’s stated mission is “to foster collaborative storytelling and shared‑world experiences for fans of *The Elder Scrolls* universe, while respecting the intellectual‑property rights of the original developers.” Core activities include:  

* Publishing fan‑created sourcebooks, adventure modules, and character sheets under a non‑commercial licence.  
* Hosting regular online play‑throughs via platforms such as Discord and Roll20.  
* Organizing annual conventions and regional meet‑ups that feature panels, game‑master workshops, and community awards.  

The public Discord server lists several thousand members. The community is primarily English‑speaking, with participants from Europe, North America, and Oceania.  

ERSC does not operate as a commercial publisher; all content is released under a non‑commercial Creative Commons‑type licence, and the organization relies on voluntary contributions and modest donation streams. A full overview is available on the society’s website (elder‑scrolls‑rpg.org), which includes its charter, recent releases, and contact information.
