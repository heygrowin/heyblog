## Overview  

Free habit‑tracker apps vary in the features they keep available without a subscription. The most common criteria users consider are:

* **Regional availability** – whether the app can be downloaded from the major app stores in a given country.  
* **Habit‑count limits** – does the free tier allow an unlimited number of habits, or does it impose a ceiling?  
* **Reminders and streak visualisation** – are push notifications and streak indicators included?  
* **Cross‑device sync** – can data be kept consistent across phones, tablets, and web browsers?  
* **Privacy and data export** – where is the data stored, is it encrypted, and can it be exported?  

The tables below summarise the five apps that regularly appear in independent reviews and that offer a functional free tier as of August 2026. Availability information is based on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store listings at that time; any regional restrictions noted are taken from the store’s “Availability” sections or from the developers’ help pages. Prices are shown in USD; local taxes or currency conversion may affect the amount shown in other markets.

## Habit‑count limits in the free tier  

| App | Free‑tier habit limit | Practical impact |
|-----|----------------------|------------------|
| **Loop – Habit Tracker** | Unlimited | Users can add as many habits as needed; the list simply scrolls. |
| **Habitica** | Unlimited | Habits, dailies, and to‑dos are all unlimited; the RPG‑style UI may become crowded with many items. |
| **HabitBull** | Unlimited | Each habit appears on its own card; the interface remains usable even with dozens of habits. |
| **TickTick** | Up to 5 habit lists (≈ 20‑30 habits total) | Users must organise habits into a maximum of five lists; exceeding the total count requires deleting or merging habits. |
| **HabitShare** | Unlimited personal habits; 3 shared habits | Personal tracking is unrestricted, but only three habits can be shared with friends without paying. |

**Takeaway:** If the primary requirement is to track a large number of distinct habits without any cap, Loop, Habitica, and HabitBull meet that need. TickTick and HabitShare impose structural limits that may require users to prioritise or prune habits.

## Cross‑device sync (free tier)  

| App | Sync availability | Sync method | Platforms linked |
|-----|-------------------|------------|------------------|
| **Loop – Habit Tracker** | No automatic cloud sync | Manual export/import of a JSON backup file (accessed via the app’s menu) | Android & iOS (separate installations) |
| **Habitica** | Yes | Account‑based sync on Habitica’s servers (TLS 1.2+) | Android, iOS, Web (any browser) |
| **HabitBull** | Yes | Encrypted backup stored in Google Drive (Android) or iCloud (iOS) | Android, iOS; read‑only web view |
| **TickTick** | Yes | Proprietary cloud service; data encrypted in transit | Android, iOS, Web, macOS, Windows |
| **HabitShare** | Yes | Account‑based sync on HabitShare’s servers | Android, iOS, Web |

If seamless sync across devices is essential, Habitica, TickTick, and HabitShare provide it without charge. Loop’s design is deliberately offline‑first; users who prefer local control can still move data between devices by manually handling the JSON backup.

## Decision framework  

When choosing a free habit‑tracker, the following factors often carry different weight for different users:

| Criterion | Why it matters | How the apps compare (free tier) |
|-----------|----------------|---------------------------------|
| **Unlimited habit count** | Allows tracking of many projects without pruning | Loop, Habitica, HabitBull – unlimited; TickTick – 5 lists; HabitShare – unlimited personal but only 3 shared |
| **Reminder flexibility** | Supports multiple daily prompts for the same habit | Habitica – multiple; Loop – one per habit; HabitBull & TickTick – up to 3; HabitShare – one |
| **Cross‑device sync** | Keeps data consistent when using phone and computer | Habitica, TickTick, HabitShare – automatic; Loop – manual; HabitBull – cloud backup (requires Google/iCloud) |
| **No ads** | Reduces visual clutter and data‑collection concerns | Loop – none; Habitica – ads (free tier) but optional removal via in‑app purchase of “Gem” cosmetics; HabitBull – ads; TickTick – ads; HabitShare – ads |
| **Data privacy** | Determines who can access your habit information | Loop – local only; others – cloud with standard encryption |

By scoring each app against the criteria that matter most to a particular user, the “best” free habit‑tracker can be identified without presenting a single app as universally superior.
